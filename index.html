A CORE INVENTORY SOURCE

Facebook Marketplace is part of AvLü.

Marketplace sourcing strategy ↗

AvLü's private source-search code already includes Facebook Marketplace for vehicles and equipment. Real buyer opportunities are often buried in incomplete private-seller listings, not just auction catalogs and dealer inventory systems. We want a consistent way to review those listings before committing capital.

01 / FIND

Find the opportunity

Buyer-provided Marketplace listings, auctions, dealer pages and other permitted sources enter a dated research record.

02 / CHECK

Confirm the evidence

Check vehicle VIN or equipment serial, exact configuration, seller claims, ownership and condition, without inventing missing details.

03 / REVIEW

Know what is unsupported

Consider recon, transport, fees and real comparable-sale evidence. A Marketplace ask is not realized resale profit.

Access boundary: Marketplace research and manual listing intake are part of the product direction. AvLü does not currently claim a live Meta-approved API feed, unattended Facebook scraping, automated seller messaging or resale posting. Future collection depends on authorized access and privacy safeguards. Read the source and permissions plan ↗
