AVLU™ / MARKETPLACE + AUCTION INTELLIGENCE / v2.5.0

From messy listing
to defensible decision.

I'm building AvLü for buyers evaluating vehicles, compact equipment and attachments across Facebook Marketplace, private sellers, dealer listings and auctions. The workflow separates seller claims from verified identity, condition, real costs and resale evidence before a buyer decides whether to pursue a listing. The original agricultural-auction work and automotive functionality remain central.

01 / MORE THAN A GENERAL AI ANSWER

AI can suggest a value. AvLü checks what supports the decision.

AI can interpret listings and help build tools. AvLü's existing review flow keeps source claims, proof gaps, costs and decisions together, including a HOLD when needed. Continuous source monitoring and actual-sale calibration are still development goals.

A CORE INVENTORY SOURCE

Facebook Marketplace is part of AvLü.

Marketplace sourcing strategy ↗

AvLü's private source-search code already includes Facebook Marketplace for vehicles and equipment. Real buyer opportunities are often buried in incomplete private-seller listings, not just auction catalogs and dealer inventory systems. We want a consistent way to review those listings before committing capital.

01 / FIND

Find the opportunity

Buyer-provided Marketplace listings, auctions, dealer pages and other permitted sources enter a dated research record.

02 / CHECK

Confirm the evidence

Check vehicle VIN or equipment serial, exact configuration, seller claims, ownership and condition, without inventing missing details.

03 / REVIEW

Know what is unsupported

Consider recon, transport, fees and real comparable-sale evidence. A Marketplace ask is not realized resale profit.

Access boundary: Marketplace research and manual listing intake are part of the product direction. AvLü does not currently claim a live Meta-approved API feed, unattended Facebook scraping, automated seller messaging or resale posting. Future collection depends on authorized access and privacy safeguards. Read the source and permissions plan ↗

THE ORIGINAL PROBLEM

Auction intelligence is core to AvLü.

Full auction research ↗

The work began with real equipment inventory and the difference between what auction buyers paid and what a unit might actually bring after fees, freight, condition work and time on the market. The original research includes 71 recorded inventory lines representing 79 units. Their projected retail spread is not documented realized profit.

01 / SOURCE

Find the exact lot

Archive event, auctioneer, date, lot, stock, serial and the original condition claims.

02 / ALL-IN COST

Include real fees

Separate hammer, buyer premiums, applicable taxes, loadout, freight and recon. Unknown costs remain open.

03 / SOLD EVIDENCE

Do not trust asking prices

Compare genuine sale-result observations, model/fitment and location without presenting a weak median as a guaranteed resale.

04 / OUTCOME

Measure actual margin

Connect an acquisition to its final sale and attributable costs when proof is available. This link is not yet calibrated.

AGRICULTURAL EQUIPMENT AND ATTACHMENTS

Machine identity matters more than a listing title.

Agriculture research brief ↗
Initial narrow policy

Non-powered compact attachments: eligible forks, buckets, root rakes, fixed blades and snow pushers. All current automated buying remains disabled.

Broader equipment research

Skid steers, compact loaders, backhoes, powered attachments, pressure washers and farm machinery. Research scope does not grant purchase authority.

Separate service R&D

Earlier equipment service-readiness design covers parts, technician preparation, machine configuration and confirmed recovery. Not a deployed service system.
