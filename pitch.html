04 / PROBLEM

A low listing price is not a profitable acquisition.

01

Incomplete assets

Missing lot identity, configuration, condition, ownership and fitment evidence.

02

Unseen expenses

Buyer premiums, taxes, transportation, reconditioning, selling costs and holding exposure.

03

Uncertain exits

Advertised resale amounts are not completed sales or realized owner profits.

The first product question: which listings deserve more attention, and which should be held or rejected?
