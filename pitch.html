04 / WHY IT IS NOT JUST AI

The value is the workflow around the answer.

GENERAL AI

Can give an estimate

Summarizes listings, writes checklists, performs math and can help build custom software.

AV LÜ™ THESIS

Maintains evidence and controls

Source lineage, exact-asset proof, assumptions, revision history, consistent HOLD logic and eventually matched realized outcomes.

Nothing prevents another team from building similar software. The commercial question is whether AvLü is faster, safer and cheaper than maintaining an equivalent workflow.
