## Suggested delivery

I would lead with the working prototype and realistic proof limitations, then use the fictional calculator and unit-economics page to demonstrate what the software and business model would do with assumptions. I would not promise fully autonomous buying, paid subscriptions, protected profits or a category-leading data moat until the necessary evidence exists.

For a reviewer with only three minutes, link [Investor Experience](../investors.html) rather than sending the complete technical README first.
