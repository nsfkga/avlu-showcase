# Equipment and auction case studies | What the evidence really says

**Public, sanitized research v1.0.0 | October 14, 2026 | Nathaniel Schroeder**

This document presents **three different evidence types**, not three profitable AvLü trades. It intentionally omits original private counterparties, raw invoices, private lot-to-unit claims and confidential acquisition math.

## Case A: A real early-2026 equipment inventory portfolio

**Source category:** retained first-party auction purchase ledger and inventory summary.  
**Recorded scope:** **71 line items, 79 units**, with individual equipment categories, hammer/acquisition costs, fees and planned retail values.  
**Included categories:** root rakes, flail mowing equipment, commercial pressure washers and other compact equipment/attachments.

**What was established:** aggregate first-party acquisition and actual all-in cost information were recovered from a historical business workbook summary. These were not merely invented listings.

**What was not established:** the retained sales log was empty at the summary date. The modeled retail spread therefore cannot be called realized profit, and unit-level sale proceeds, exact expenses, transaction dates and holding time remain missing.

**Further unit-level limitation:** a historical root-rake sample remains on HOLD because the original exact lot, unit configuration, unit invoice matching and resale outcome have not all been reconciled. Generic manufacturer pages cannot substitute for an identified unit.

**Takeaway:** AvLü needs an evidence chain from the original acquisition source to a specific completed sale, not a workbook that reports a theoretical retail value as revenue.

## Case B: Public pallet-fork and root-rake results

**Source category:** public auction sale-result research, not first-party sales.

The retained private September/October analysis reviewed **22 candidate results**: **18 pallet-fork**, **3 root-rake** and **1 blade** observations. In the original pallet-fork subset, the reported descriptive median was **$300.50** and the 75th-percentile value **$500**, but **nine of 18** came from one location/date cluster. A later source-trace pass recovered three exact lot pages without counting new outcomes.

Those statistics are **descriptions of a weak heterogeneous sample**, not validated resale prices for any particular offered pallet forks. Mounts, capacities, assembly completeness, brand, condition and distance differ. A category-level model that converts the $300.50 median directly into a bid for an unidentified unit would be unreliable.

Examples of publicly referenced results:
- [48-inch Wolverine pallet-fork lot, September 12, 2026, reported $450](https://hibid.com/en/lot/319738485/2026-wolverine-skid-steer-48--pallet-forks)
- [42-inch Tomahawk pallet-fork lot, September 19, 2026, reported $600](https://hibid.com/www.rgmongler.com/lot/322124571/tomahawk-skid-loader-pallet-forks-42--new?ref=catalog)
- [Root-rake lot, September 1, 2026, reported $920](https://hibid.com/lot/317505117/pabreak-root-rake-attachment)

These are independent **public research leads**, not proof of owned resale transactions, attributable settlement or interchangeable comps.

**Status:** category calibration remains incomplete. Current acquisition approvals: **zero**.

## Case C: A "snow pusher" title conceals a powered attachment

**Public lot:** [Wolverine DB-15-84W](https://hibid.com/www.heretn.com/lot/323004423/wolverine-db-15-84w-skid-steer-snow-pusher), lot 11096, reported $850 Price Realized on September 21, 2026 in Wisconsin.

**Public terms:** auctioneer lists a 10% buyer premium. Actual tax, payment channel and buyer freight are transaction-specific, and the final buyer invoice was not reviewed.

**Configuration problem:** [manufacturer specifications](https://usawolverine.com/html/product/205.html) classify DB-15-84W as hydraulically powered. Another [Wolverine SP-series product](https://global.usawolverine.com/products/snow-pusher) is a different family. The lot's words "snow pusher" alone do not prove equivalence.

**Result:** the identified hydraulic DB model is **excluded from the initial non-powered policy**. The price-realized observation remains a research result, not a supported bid.

## Proof hierarchy demonstrated

1. **Auction listed:** observed opportunity, not an outcome.
2. **Auction ended:** event closed, final price might still be inaccessible.
3. **Price Realized shown:** labeled public outcome, with lot-level scope and platform limits.
4. **Buyer settled:** actual paid all-in cost with applicable transaction evidence.
5. **Seller resold:** documented actual proceeds and dates.
6. **Realized contribution/net:** complete attributable economics with reviewable evidence.

A source can support one step without supporting the next.

## Next honest experiment

Recover a few **fully matched and legally usable purchase-to-resale chains** in one small category. Freeze the decision-time observation, reconcile all actual purchase costs, then assess realized sale economics. Until then, AvLü should not claim proven profitable acquisition predictions.
