# Autonomous acquisition | Engineering and release boundaries

**v2.1.0 | October 14, 2026 | Nathaniel Schroeder**

## What autonomous means in the long-term plan

**Permitted repeated observation:** monitor eligible inventory sources on an authorized schedule and preserve changed prices, availability and condition claims.

**Evidence organization:** reconcile identity, provenance, configured options, inspection, costs and matched market observations.

**Decision simulation:** evaluate a clearly versioned policy and save the explanation, gaps, and conservative next action.

**Human-controlled operating expansion:** only after real-world calibration, legal review, financing, reserve exposure and execution/rollback safeguards, consider narrow live transactions with explicit authorization.

The product is **not** currently placing automated bids, purchases, purchase orders, seller contacts, or payments.

## Four reference states

| State | Intended interpretation | Does it spend money? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOLD | Required evidence or costs are unresolved | No |
| REJECT | Candidate is outside the supported policy or economics | No |
| COUNTER | Reference ceiling suggests a negotiation path, subject to sufficient evidence | No |
| APPROVE | Reference policy test satisfied for the frozen input | **No**: classification is not buying authority |

The tested reference engine always maintains a non-executable boundary. Even an `APPROVE` classification is **not** an accepted purchase authorization.

## What already exists

- Reference engine v0.4.0 with **51/51** reported passing synthetic checks.
- Explicit narrow non-powered attachment scope, with powered and automotive live buying excluded.
- Tested shadow-only intent and audit behavior at the source layer.
- A 30-case public shadow observation window with **30/30 HOLD**.
- Input validation, source evidence gating and inspection protocol testing.
- Persistent owner research work and a recorded PostgreSQL restore rehearsal using fictional data.

These are meaningful software and control milestones. None is evidence that the business successfully bought and resold inventory autonomously.

## Why live acquisition is blocked

**Evidence:** no approved field inspection captures, not enough outcome-linked/full-economics cases, no qualifying sealed APPROVE/COUNTER sample and no independent real acquisition approvals.

**Calibration:** selected categories lack credible matched completed-sale results. The underlying model must not treat an asking price as settlement proceeds.

**Legal and financial controls:** permitted transaction role, jurisdiction, available capital, reserves, stop-loss, contract obligations and any protection claims still require review and approval.

**Production operations:** authenticated owner acceptance, recoverable backups, protected transaction adapters, permission scoping, emergency stop, rollback and revocation edge cases still need deployed verification.

**Explicit release:** test success must never silently turn on live execution or a profit-protection promise.

## Separate proposed Protection™ concept

Profit Protection™ is a **named research and product concept, currently inactive**. The existence of reference payout math does not establish insurance, a legally approved guarantee, a funded loss reserve, policy coverage or customer entitlement. No user should rely on it.

## Questions worth asking now

1. Which category has enough matched outcomes to justify conservative calibration?
2. Where could permitted, event-driven price observations replace brittle manual collection?
3. How should audit evidence be tied to a frozen policy version so history cannot be rewritten?
4. Which controls must be external to the policy engine before capital can be deployed?
5. Where would a human-in-the-loop pilot produce decisive evidence before a costly system integration?

## Auction category boundary and permitted observations

The first proposed autonomous category is **selected non-powered compact attachments**, not every vehicle, loader or agricultural machine. Root rakes, snow pushers and blades require physical/configuration proof because similar titles can denote powered hydraulic components.

The development goal is permissioned recurring lot observation, accurate fee/settlement evidence, conservative comparison and matched resale outcomes. The published Watchlist is a research tool, **not proof of an always-updating external market feed**. Neither a reference APPROVE nor a closed auction authorizes any bid or purchase.
