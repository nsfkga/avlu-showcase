## First, what it is and why it is different

**AvLü is a working pre-acquisition review application** for vehicles, equipment and attachments discovered on Facebook Marketplace, at auctions, through dealers and in private sales. It keeps seller claims, missing proof, conditional total costs and documented next actions together.

**Versus normal AI:** the proposed product value is the persistent case/evidence and decision workflow, not unique prompting. Source automation and verified realized-profit feedback remain limited or planned.

**Versus competitors:** Tractor Zoom and Anvil already provide valuation/appraisal and dealer workflows, while EquipmentWatch provides sold/auction data. I am testing a narrower **proof-before-offer** process for poorly documented mixed-source inventory. It needs direct pilot evidence before claiming superiority.

## The founder's thesis

Dealers and other resale operators can review attractive asking prices much faster than they can assemble trustworthy evidence on identity, condition, total cost and feasible resale. I want AvLü to reduce that gap before acquisition decisions, then improve its calibration from actual, matched transactions.

I am not claiming this workflow is wholly new or impossible to imitate. The value proposition must be demonstrated in lower effort, better diligence, auditable decisions and justified pricing.

## The stage I am at

- I have a working published application with guided intake and protected owner functionality.
- I have built and tested additional reference logic for acquisition decisions, data persistence and shadow-only controls.
- I have source-level and synthetic QA evidence, including 2,000 synthetic regression cases.
- I do **not** have enough properly linked realized purchase-to-resale outcomes to claim validated profit prediction.
- No autonomous purchases, binding bids or profit-protection coverage are active.

## What I need mentors to challenge

**Is the first monetizable workflow small, painful and repeatable enough?** Specifically, can one buyer go from messy listing to an evidence-backed *next action* measurably faster or with fewer missed risks than with existing tools? If that result is weak, the correct response is to narrow or change the product.

## Start with the origins that explain the product

This is **not primarily a car-pricing chatbot**. My work began with auction-purchased farm-adjacent and compact-equipment inventory, buyer cost analysis and proposed resale pricing. The retained historical inventory includes **79 units**, and missing completed resale data is a central motivation for the product's outcome-linking direction.

Read [Agriculture](AGRICULTURAL_EQUIPMENT.md), [Auction Intelligence](AUCTION_INTELLIGENCE.md), [Actual Research Cases](AG_AUCTION_CASE_STUDIES.md), [Timeline](PROJECT_ORIGINS_TIMELINE.md) and [Data Automation](AUCTION_DATA_AUTOMATION.md) before the rest of the architecture overview.
