## Financial integrity

The 79-unit historic equipment record supports real **aggregate acquisition costs**; its projected retail values are **not revenue or profit**. Third-party auction price-realized labels also are **not** evidence that AvLü or its founder resold those items.

Projected SaaS MRR, ARR, CAC, gross margin, break-even and runway are **scenarios only**. Pricing has not been validated with paying customers in these public materials.

Do not combine SaaS revenues with founder-owned resale, dealer gross profits or future Parts Commerce sales. Those are different business activities with different capital and risk profiles.

## Principal risks

1. **Competitive overlap:** Equipment valuation, auctions, inventory and AI assistance are already well-served markets. The pre-purchase evidence workflow must show incremental value.
2. **Data rights and pricing:** Commercial source access may be costly, incomplete or restricted. Publicly visible pages are not automatically legal commercial feeds.
3. **Missing outcomes:** Reported auction sale prices do not show attributable reseller cost, seller proceeds, recon or holding time.
4. **Physical-unit uncertainty:** Titles and model families conceal fitment, hydraulic configuration and condition differences.
5. **Founder-dependent operations:** Detailed evidence review and bespoke integrations may make SaaS margins lower than modeled.
6. **Acquisition and retention:** The first dollar of recurring revenue and first repeat renewal are not yet evidenced.
7. **Safety/legal:** Binding offers, autonomously using funds and any protection promise create obligations not cleared by synthetic QA.
8. **Source reconciliation:** Later unpublished inspection/market functionality needs safe merge, storage verification, privacy and owner acceptance.

## Funding-related diligence

The $100,000 / six-to-nine-month plan is a **planning example**. Before funds are accepted, I need a real spending schedule, data license and integration quotes, founder responsibilities, legal formation/ownership assessment, security policies, business insurance as applicable and a financing instrument reviewed by professionals.

A SAFE, equity financing, loan, grant and customer-funded pilot do not have interchangeable repayment and ownership consequences. No valuation cap, ownership percentage, return or exit is offered in this public record.

## What would change the investment case

- At least 3–5 real paying pilots, with an independently observable conversion or renewal.
- Actual account-level gross contribution after licensed data and human support.
- A narrow permissioned source agreement and reproducible equipment-comparison quality.
- Matched completed-sale case records that enable honest outcome calibration.
- Evidence that buyers make decisions faster or with fewer consequential missed items.
- A verified production and recovery posture, including owner acceptance.

**Decision principle:** a compelling application demo is not an investor return. A credible paid workflow, legally usable data and durable software economics together make a business worth investigating.
