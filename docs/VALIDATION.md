# AvLü™ | Validation and evidence notes

**Version v1.0.0 | October 14, 2026**

I separate correctness of calculations from business performance. A formula can calculate consistently and still be wrong about resale value if its input evidence is poor.

## Historical calculation audit

- Screening examples audited: **150**.
- Reported arithmetic mismatches: **0**.
- Marked for further investigation: **7**.
- Acquisition-approved in that audit: **0**.
- These outcomes are from an internal consistency and screening review, **not** a set of completed purchases.

## What these figures do not prove

- They do not prove buyers will realize the projected spread or return.
- They do not prove a particular user saved time or money.
- They do not validate the availability of a live dealer integration or data-license rights.
- They do not show independently validated or recurring commercial revenue.
- They do not establish that autonomous bids or purchases are safely enabled.

## How I plan to test product value

I want to evaluate a bounded user workflow using representative records and compare time spent, missing information detected, confidence in the recommendation, false-positive holds, and the alignment between prior estimates and verified outcomes. Any public results should state the sampling method and applicable limitations.

Customer-specific economics, private interview details, identifiable seller or dealer data, exact validation fixtures, and operational control thresholds remain private.
