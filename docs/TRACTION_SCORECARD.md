## Commercial validation

| Metric investors ask for | Public evidence status | What is needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paying SaaS customers | **Not independently verified** | Actual executed agreements, invoices, collections |
| Monthly recurring revenue / ARR | **Not independently verified** | Active contract-level recurring revenue reconciliation |
| Paid pilot conversions | **Not established** | Signed scopes, received payment, pilot outcome reports |
| Customer acquisition cost (CAC) | **Not measured** | Founder hours, tooling, travel, marketing, conversions |
| Customer churn and retention | **Not measured** | Renewals, cancellations and cohorts |
| Fully loaded gross margin | **Not measured** | Actual source rights, processing, support and infrastructure cost |
| Customer outcome improvement | **Not established** | Side-by-side trial against current buyer workflow |
| Realized resale-profit calibration | **Blocked by incomplete matched outcomes** | Same-unit purchase and sale receipts plus all attributable expenses |
| Authorized unattended collection | **Not established** | Source terms, production scheduler, freshness and correctness checks |

Do not translate "not independently verified" into a categorical claim that no revenue exists outside these public records. It means the public showcase has no evidence adequate to substantiate a number.

## Illustrative goals, not progress already achieved

The first customer funnel to test is **50 qualified accounts, 15 demonstrations, five paid pilots, and three recurring conversions**. The $199/$499/from-$999 subscriptions, $1,500 fully loaded CAC, 5% churn, 72.9% gross margin and $100,000 initial funding plan are *planning assumptions*, not operating metrics.

## Next evidence milestones

- Confirm a narrow customer with a frequently repeated acquisition-review decision.
- Demonstrate legally usable equipment/auction-source coverage.
- Record a first real, paid, limited pilot with a blinded baseline comparison.
- Measure repeat customer behavior and the full time cost of founder assistance.
- Reconcile attributable original-unit acquisition, expenses and actual resale documentation.
- Safely release source code with production owner acceptance, restored backups and rollback readiness.

**The reason for the scorecard:** showing what has not been proved is as important as showing what the prototype and tests have accomplished.
