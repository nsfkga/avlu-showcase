## What AvLü is

**AvLü is acquisition intelligence for buyers evaluating equipment, attachments and used vehicles across auctions, Facebook Marketplace, dealerships and private sellers.** The software helps them distinguish a promising listing from a purchase supported by the available facts, relevant market evidence and realistic all-in costs.

**I built an actual Auction Market Engine**, not simply an AI-assisted valuation presentation. The published tracker at [getavlu.com](https://getavlu.com/#market-engine) displays **real reported equipment auction results**, source dates, sample counts, medians, price ranges and filters. A separate private engine contains source-policy controls, conditional acquisition economics, evidence-based HOLD decisions and outcome records.

**Price the purchase. Preserve the proof.** This addresses the moment *before* equipment becomes purchased inventory.

## 1. Why AvLü is more than just normal AI

General AI can interpret listings and calculate conditional margins. AvLü has already built a **repeatable, source-linked market observation process** rather than relying on a generated price.

- **Observed, attributable prices:** Published source observations are classified as reported auction hammer results with capture dates, source links and clear sample limitations.
- **Physical and economic proof gates:** The source-tested private engine distinguishes price types and can HOLD when required identity, condition or purchase-cost evidence remains unknown.
- **Review history and outcomes:** Original forecasts, decision records and completed-trade inputs are different data types, allowing later comparison without changing the historical prediction.
- **Controlled source operation:** Public captures run at most hourly on activity, with durable leases and partial-source visibility. This is a managed workflow, not unrestricted automated browsing.

As of October 22, 2026, the **published v2.18.6 tracker** had recorded eight equipment families and 66 eligible filter cohorts in a real production acceptance, with two of three source pages covered. Those are real observations in a **partial, source-specific sample**, not 66 verified transactions by AvLü.

The broader private acquisition engine is source-tested but not fully owner-accepted or authorized to execute purchases. A later **v2.19.0** individual reported-price chart remains an unpublished candidate.

## 2. How AvLü is positioned against competitors

**Tractor Zoom Pro/Anvil Pro already offer serious dealership tools and equipment-price intelligence. EquipmentWatch already provides long auction/resale histories and APIs.** AvLü is not trying to outsize their databases immediately.

My initial commercial focus is the **independent buyer's pre-purchase evaluation**: gather eligible source observations, compare an appropriately identified unit, identify costs and missing proof, and keep a reviewable next action before spending money.

- **Versus an equipment-market database:** The system is intended to put those observations into **this buyer's acquisition cost and evidence review**, not merely display a historical price.
- **Versus enterprise dealer platforms:** The product targets fragmented buying opportunities without requiring a full CRM, DMS or quoting-system replacement.
- **Versus custom AI and spreadsheets:** I am offering maintained, source-linked price records, repeatable cost/condition review and explicit decision-state controls rather than starting a new ad hoc estimate each time.

One specific distinction is Anvil Pro's documented April 2026 sales-quote Margin Targets feature: it provides color-coded guidance and does not block a below-target quote. AvLü's source-tested pre-purchase evaluation can instead return **HOLD** when a required input is missing. This describes two individual workflow stages, not every incumbent capability.

**The investable question:** Will buyers pay for this combined market-observation and acquisition-review workflow because it reduces manual reconciliation or consequential errors? That needs paid pilots and observed economics.

## The buyer and the benefit

The initial customer is an independent equipment/attachment reseller or smaller dealer evaluating inconsistent auction and private-sale inventory. Automotive functionality remains supported. The first proposed autonomous category is limited to selected non-powered attachments, with purchasing disabled.

The intended benefit is **better coordination with less complication**: keep the listing, cost exposure, missing proof, original forecast, and later result together rather than reconstruct them across browsing tabs, chats, and spreadsheets.

The next commercial test is a paid comparison against the buyer's existing process, measuring time, material omissions, false warnings, repeat use, and cost to serve. Customer savings and superiority over alternatives are not yet established.

## Revenue model

The core model is subscription software, not financing customer inventory or collecting the buyer's resale proceeds.

| Offer under evaluation | Proposed price |
| --- | ---: |
| Independent | $199/month or $1,990/year |
| Dealer Team | $499/month |

These are hypotheses, not confirmed offers or paid subscriptions. Data rights, plan features, usage limits, and delivery costs must be established before customer commitments.

A hypothetical 25-account mix generates $8,975 MRR and $107,700 annualized ARR. Assumed direct costs of $2,430/month leave $6,545 gross profit. After $9,000/month of modeled fixed costs, the scenario loses $2,455/month before additional acquisition costs and taxes. Approximate blended break-even is 35 accounts. ## What funding would accomplish

The illustrative $100,000 plan allocates $40,000 to engineering/founder QA, $20,000 to data access and integration, $15,000 to pilots/onboarding, $10,000 to security/infrastructure, $10,000 to legal/accounting, and $5,000 to contingency.

The objective is a protected integrated product, authorized source access, paid pilots, measured customer value, and actual delivery costs. This is not money raised, a securities offer, or guaranteed runway. [Funding plan](FUNDING_PLAN.md).

## The next business milestone

The proposed founder-led funnel is 50 qualified accounts, 15 demonstrations, five paid pilots, and three recurring conversions. Those are targets, not traction. Recurring value must be supported by voluntary repeat use, customer economics, and maintainable operations.

Continuous unattended Marketplace sourcing, automatic purchases, Profit Protection™, and independently verified profit prediction are not active features. The first commercial test is whether the acquisition-review workflow earns its subscription.
