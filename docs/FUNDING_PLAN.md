# AvLü™ | Illustrative $100,000 funding plan

**Investor planning hypothesis | v1.0.0 | October 15, 2026**  
**Owner: Nathaniel Schroeder**

## The ask being evaluated

I am modeling **$100,000 in initial funding** to finance a controlled six-to-nine-month validation period. This is not an executed raise, accepted investor term, legal solicitation, or guaranteed operating runway. Spending and timing depend on actual quotes, founder compensation, receipts and product progress.

## Proposed uses

| Purpose | Allocation | What it is intended to accomplish |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Engineering, founder work and quality assurance | **$40,000** | Reconcile source branches; maintain an audited owner workflow; test matching, storage, interfaces and access controls |
| Data licenses and integrations | **$20,000** | Validate lawful auction and equipment feeds; establish dated history and original-lot matching |
| Paid-pilot customer development and onboarding | **$15,000** | Founder-led demos, controlled trial setup, service documentation, measured customer outcomes |
| Security, cloud hosting and operations | **$10,000** | Protected access, testing, reliable operations, recovery rehearsal, monitoring and support |
| Legal, accounting and data rights | **$10,000** | Commercial terms, ownership and licensing, business formation/financing review where needed |
| Contingency | **$5,000** | Source and integration delays, technical or compliance costs |
| **Total** | **$100,000** | **Planning allocation, not spending already incurred** |

The $40,000 engineering allocation includes **lean founder labor**. It cannot be presented as sufficient to pay multiple full-time market-rate salaries for a year. Do not treat the allocations as actual vendor bids.

## Milestone-based capital deployment

| Window | Proposed release focus | Proof that unlocks further investment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Months 1–2 | Protect source history, complete safe application integration, narrow one buyer/category, validate source terms | Release-gate receipts and permissioned source plan; no destructive production migration |
| Months 3–4 | Onboard first paying pilot(s), measure current process, test actual decision-time workflows | Signed paid trial, baseline and repeat-usage observations, identified economic buyer |
| Months 5–6 | Improve category comparisons and real cost/outcome tracking, test price acceptance | Verified comparable provenance and at least one legitimately matched purchase-to-resale record where available |
| Months 7–9 | Evaluate retention, servicing cost, product/channel economics and next financing | Credible paid conversions/renewals, unit economics, measured time or error impact, documented stop/go decision |

These windows are **targets**, not commitments that the product or buyer participation can be forced on a calendar.

## Non-goals for this first round

- Do not finance speculative inventory purchasing from this SaaS runway.
- Do not promise a thousand realized-profit records or automate binding bids before data and authority exist.
- Do not build the full automotive dealer suite, ag service platform, parts marketplace and acquisition engine simultaneously.
- Do not buy uncertain data rights or perform unrestricted scraping.
- Do not claim a regulated protection or underwriting product is live.
- Do not substitute synthetic QA outcomes for commercial traction.

## Investor release gates

Before investing materially beyond the first bounded phase, I would want **authorized data access, technical safety, at least three-to-five paying pilots, repeated use, a defensible pricing signal, and a costed plan for maintaining each account**.

A successful software deployment without paying customers is technical progress, not product-market fit. A paid pilot that requires substantial hidden founder labor may be a consulting engagement rather than scalable software.

## Financing choices are separate decisions

Equity, a SAFE, grants, repayable loans and customer prepayments affect ownership and cash obligations differently. A $100,000 example at a $2 million post-money cap may imply 5% initial economic ownership before dilution under certain SAFE terms, but **this is not a proposed valuation or offer** and depends on actual legal documents. Get professional advice before setting terms.

## Runway caveat

Six to nine months assumes the combined spending plan and timing are realistic. The cash plan must be updated for actual payroll, source-license minimums, annual prepaid expenses, collections, taxes and debt service. An attractive ARR illustration is not cash in the bank.
