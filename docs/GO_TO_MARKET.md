# AvLü™ | First-customer and pilot go-to-market plan

**Founder-led commercialization hypothesis | v1.1.0 | October 15, 2026**  
**Owner: Nathaniel Schroeder**

## Start with one economic buyer

My initial outreach will focus on **independent used equipment and attachment resellers** who repeatedly encounter inconsistent auction catalogs, private listings and uncertain buyer costs. The adjacent category is small dealer buying teams. Larger dealer-system integrations are a later stage with longer support and sales cycles.

Customer research and demonstrations are not the same as paid acquisition. My reported 31 dealer meetings and the earlier 23 completed I-Corps records do not establish paid subscriptions, pilots or contracted revenue.

## Discovery channels versus customer-acquisition channels

**Inventory-sourcing channels:** Facebook Marketplace, permitted equipment and farm auctions, Craigslist/private listings and dealer inventories. These supply candidate opportunities *for users to evaluate*, not automatic customers or verified resale prices. The source-search component recognizes Facebook in both vehicle and equipment workflows, but there is **no production Facebook synchronization**.

**Customer-acquisition channels:** direct introductions through dealer/equipment networks, I-Corps discovery follow-ups where appropriate, demos and authorized pilot recruitment. Posting links on Facebook Marketplace or collecting listings does not turn those listing sellers into SaaS subscribers.

A bounded demonstration can compare **one recent Marketplace listing and one auction or dealer listing** against the buyer's existing process, when the buyer has access/rights to use them. Count the number of material gaps found, reviewer minutes, false holds, repeat use and willingness to pay.

The product should support **buyer-submitted source URLs and permitted manual captures first**, followed by approved feeds where available. Do not treat Facebook account sign-in, scraping or seller messaging automation as an approved acquisition channel for AvLü. [Marketplace workflow](FACEBOOK_MARKETPLACE_STRATEGY.md).

## Hypothetical conversion funnel

| Activity | Planning target | What qualifies it |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Qualified accounts | **50** | Buyer evaluates enough mixed-source opportunities and owns or influences the budget |
| Substantive demonstrations | **15** | A real past decision and current workflow can be reviewed |
| Paid controlled pilots | **5** | Written scope and paid agreement, not complimentary access |
| Recurring conversions | **3** | Customer independently agrees to subscribe after seeing measured value |

The funnel implies a **30% demo/qualified rate**, roughly **33% paid pilot/demo rate**, and **60% subscription/pilot rate**. These are **hypotheses for testing**, not observed AvLü conversion metrics.

Five pilots at an illustrative $1,000 each yield **$5,000 one-time pilot revenue**, not $5,000 recurring revenue. Three Independent subscriptions would yield **$597 MRR** at the proposed $199 price; any mix of higher tiers must be supported by actual contracted plan features.

## The demonstration should be concrete

1. Ask the buyer to select a recent appropriately authorized auction or attachment listing.
2. Record the original buyer decision and time spent, using information available at that time.
3. Show exact-asset identity, configuration, buyer fee terms, freight and condition unknowns, and market comparability.
4. Display a documented next action, including HOLD when necessary. Never promise a profitable trade.
5. Review false warnings, missing sources and actual effort added.
6. Ask the economic buyer for a paid, fixed-scope trial rather than another informal expression of interest.

Do not contact parties with existing do-not-contact restrictions. All trial records and any customer integrations require explicit permission.

## Proposed paid pilot terms to test

- Price: **$750–$1,500 once**, with the exact fee tied to limited review scope.
- Scope: one buyer or branch, one suitable category, approximately 5–10 decision-time opportunities.
- Duration: agreed pilot period and review session, not an indefinite trial.
- Baseline: the participant's actual current process, counted for human time.
- Results: median time, material missed evidence, false HOLDs, cost estimation error when receipts exist, repeat usage and burden of integration.
- End state: independent re-use, refusal, signed recurring contract or a documented decision to stop.

No hands-off buying, guarantees or automated seller messages.

## Sales effort and CAC

A provisional fully loaded **$1,500 CAC** is a hypothesis that should include founder time, research/prep, demos, travel, follow-up and tools. It should not be presented as a measured cost per sale.

Record every qualified prospect, meetings, time spent, paid conversion and first renewal or churn. If founder labor is doing all the work behind the scenes, account for it as a cost of acquiring and serving the customer.

## Evidence investors should receive

- Paying customer count, active contract value and invoice/collection status.
- Customer type, frequency of opportunities and actual usage over time.
- Comparisons against the prior buyer workflow with negative results included.
- Actual direct per-account data and support costs.
- Owner of the purchase decision and documented budget justification.
- Reasons people decline after seeing a demonstration.

## Decision rules

Continue if multiple customers independently pay, return without prompting and describe material value after accounting for delivery costs. Narrow, integrate into existing tools or stop if the product merely recreates existing free valuation functions or requires too much manual analysis to scale.
