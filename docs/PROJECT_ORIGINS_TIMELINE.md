# AvLü™ project evolution | Equipment, auctions and engineering

**Public, sanitized chronology v1.0.0 | October 14, 2026 | Nathaniel Schroeder**

This timeline preserves **why the product changed direction**. It does not claim each stage became a revenue-generating product.

| Period | What I was working on | The key insight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January to February 2026 | Auction-purchased equipment and attachments, actual acquisition costs, inventory tracking and planned resale prices; a historical 71-line, 79-unit portfolio | A promising retail spread is not realized profit without matched sales and all expenses |
| Spring and early summer 2026 | Automotive and equipment screening workflows, local/auction/private listing comparison, VIN and configuration evidence, protected offer / walk-away views | Weak or missing evidence must not be turned into confident purchase recommendations |
| June 2026 | Historical 150-row arithmetic audit and conservative screening outcomes | A reject/hold result can be a useful product behavior |
| Summer 2026 | Working browser prototype, backend and review flow development; dealer outreach and technical architecture | Product value depends on source organization and workflow, not merely an AI answer |
| September 2026 | Great Plains I-Corps discovery and equipment-dealer workflow research, including field-service readiness concept | Established dealers already use extensive tools; the better question is what repeated work can actually be removed |
| Late September and early October 2026 | Auction result research, strict comparable filtering, narrow non-powered attachment box, synthetic shadow window, buyer-fee normalization | Equipment description, actual configuration, price labels and settlement economics must be controlled separately |
| October 2026 | Reference decision engine, 30 sealed HOLD cases, 2,000 synthetic regression packets, owner Watchlist, PostgreSQL and protected recovery checks | Robust software tests do not substitute for matched realized outcomes or permission to buy |
| October 14, 2026 | GitHub source v2.14.0, separate unpublished Replit v2.15.2 inspection/market-research workspace, last verified publication v2.13.0 | Preserve divergent working lines before merging or publishing; never mistake source changes for deployed capabilities |

## My current synthesis

AvLü combines a **long-running equipment and auction acquisition question** with an increasingly structured **evidence-first software implementation**. Agriculture and auction sourcing are foundational to the product, automotive functionality remains supported, and service-readiness research is a separate design lane.

The near-term challenge is not another marketing layer. It is obtaining an authorized, matched **lot → actual acquisition cost → disposition proceeds and expenses** chain, and comparing the system against a real buyer's current workflow.
