# AvLü™ frequently asked questions

**v2.2.0 | October 15, 2026 | Nathaniel Schroeder**

### What will AvLü charge customers?

The proposed subscription tiers are **$199/month** for Independent users, **$499/month** for Dealer Teams, and **from $999/month** for individually scoped integrations. I am also testing **$750–$1,500** one-time pilots. No paid uptake has been verified in this showcase. Customer equipment gross resale profits are not AvLü company revenue.

### Is AvLü raising $100,000?

I am exploring a **$100,000 funding scenario** for an evidence-based six-to-nine-month build and validation period, not representing that it has already been raised. The proposed spending split is $40k engineering, $20k authorized data, $15k pilot sales/onboarding, $10k security, $10k legal/accounting and $5k contingency.

### Is there already profitable recurring revenue?

No verified paying customer count or contracted ARR is supported here. A modeled 25-account mix generates $8,975 hypothetical MRR but also shows a $2,455 monthly operating shortfall under assumed costs. Break-even near 35 accounts is an illustration, not a proven result.

### Is AvLü an actual application or an idea?

There is a working private application and a public website at https://getavlu.com. The latest verified published application is v2.13.0. The privately maintained source has later tested features that have not all passed production publication and owner acceptance.

### Does AvLü already find and buy profitable equipment automatically?

No. Authorized continuous sourcing is still a development objective, and purchases are disabled. A reference decision engine does not grant authority to submit an offer or payment.

### Has AvLü proved actual resale profits?

No. Synthetic and historical audit results are not completed transactions. Some first-party cost information and public sold-result observations exist, but fully matched purchase-to-sale proceeds and attributable costs remain insufficient.

### Can users review cars, agricultural equipment and attachments?

The prototype and research span vehicles and equipment. The **initial proposed autonomous acquisition policy** is narrower: selected non-powered compact-equipment attachments. Powered machinery and vehicles do not have released autonomous buying authority.

### What is Profit Protection™?

A planned product/control concept that is **inactive**. It is not an operating guarantee, insurance product or legally offered funded coverage.

### Why not just use ChatGPT?

A one-off answer is often possible in a GPT. The proposed difference is a maintained, permissioned, source-linked, tested decision workflow with history and measured outcomes. Whether it offers enough added value is a commercial question being tested.

### Is this GitHub repository the proprietary source?

No. It is a separately written public explanation and a fixed-state fictional demonstration. Private code, algorithms, data and operational details are excluded.

### What would a technical mentor be reviewing?

Architecture concepts, provenance strategy, category scope, outcome integrity, observed software testing and a proposed measurable pilot. A confidential code review, if appropriate, is a separate decision.

### What does the customer research establish?

The founder reports 31 dealer meetings, while a separate course log documented 23 completed discovery conversations. Research has refined the hypotheses; neither count alone establishes revenue, formal pilots or product-market fit.

### Can I evaluate the live app without access to private records?

Yes, use the public site and documented flow. The showcase is intentionally not a credentialed copy of protected owner functionality.

### Is AvLü rooted in agricultural and auction work?

Yes. Its early 2026 inventory-accounting work tracked **71 acquisition lines and 79 units** across attachments and farm-adjacent equipment, recording actual aggregate costs and planned resale. The sales ledger did not establish completed realized profits.

### Is the auction price the buyer's entire cost?

No. A "Price Realized" figure, hammer, paid invoice, buyer premium, tax, loadout, recon, freight and eventual seller proceeds are separate. Terms vary by auction, lot and method.

### Is agricultural service software already deployed?

No. Equipment Service Readiness and Recovery is a separate earlier design/research track with synthetic tests, not a verified production dealer system.

### Does AvLü already scan and synchronize auction sources continuously?

No confirmed production service does that today. The private published Watchlist and a newer separate, unpublished Replit market-research workspace are precursors, and their versions must be reconciled before claiming a live integrated system.
