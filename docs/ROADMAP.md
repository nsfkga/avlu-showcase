# Development roadmap | What comes next

**Public roadmap v2.2.0 | October 15, 2026 | Nathaniel Schroeder**

This page separates work already achieved from work proposed, under test or blocked. It is not a deployment promise.

## Phase 1: Working prototype and research foundation

**Existing / demonstrated**

- Responsive public website and guided entry flow.
- Protected owner research and review features.
- Published private Watchlist v1.0.0.
- Reference-only decision states and scoped policies.
- Historical formula audit and large synthetic QA campaign.
- Source-aware listing research and strict comparable-review holds.
- CI security, route, browser and persistence testing.

**Still required for full acceptance**

- Authenticated owner verification on the recorded published runtime.
- Explicit production recovery, backup and deploy/rollback tests.
- Stronger evidence from actual users and completed transaction outcomes.

## Phase 2: Evidence and category calibration

**Active / not ready to claim success**

- Capture verified identity and condition information for exact units.
- Reconcile actual fees, recon/repair, transport and holding time.
- Match true completed-sale outcomes to historical observations.
- Show reliable source and time confidence.
- Calibrate one small, well-supported non-powered attachment category at a time.
- Run a permitted, measurable pilot and publish both favorable and negative results.

**Hard gate:** no unsupported bid ceiling or acquisition approval from weak comparables or incomplete actual costs.

## Phase 3: Autonomous observation, not autonomous purchasing

**Planned and dependent on source rights / infrastructure**

- Authorized recurring collection and price/availability change detection.
- Durable snapshots with stale/duplicate detection.
- User-defined watch policies and priority notifications.
- Evidence updates that do not overwrite earlier observations.
- Review of operational burden and data licensing terms.

A saved Watchlist does **not** mean this fully autonomous monitoring layer is already running.

## Parallel commercial milestones

**Planning, not achieved:** validate the $199 / $499 / from-$999 monthly subscription rates; qualify 50 buyers; deliver 15 meaningful demos; target five fixed-scope paid pilots and three independent recurring conversions. Record founder sales time, source cost, support cost, missed proof, false HOLDs and observed customer benefit. The hypothetical $100,000 seed allocation supports only bounded proof, not every prospective AvLü product line.

[Commercialization](COMMERCIALIZATION.md) · [Funding and milestones](FUNDING_PLAN.md).

## Phase 4: Narrow executable acquisition

**Blocked / later**

- Professional legal/commercial classification and agreement review.
- Verified capital reserves, exposure caps, incident and loss management.
- Executable transaction integration with clear authentication, explicit release and idempotency.
- Accepted operational kill-switch, rollback, human override and audit.
- Demonstrated shadow-window outcomes and category calibration.
- Separate approval before any bid, offer or purchase authority is enabled.

## Related R&D: Equipment Service Readiness and Recovery

Earlier research and synthetic requirements work explored machine configuration, parts fitment, job preparation, technician handoffs and confirmed recovery outcomes. This is an **adjacent research track**, not the currently released acquisition product. It can be revisited only if user evidence shows a compelling service-workflow opportunity.

## Immediate decision I need help with

I need a **narrow source-and-outcome pilot** that measures improvement against an existing buyer workflow. More screens or more unverified asking-price captures will not establish realized-profit accuracy.

## Auction and agriculture: next controlled work

1. **Preserve the existing code lines:** reconcile the unpublished Replit v2.15.2 inspection/market-research worktree with GitHub v2.14.0 without overwriting owner data, then run release and production-owner acceptance gates before publication.
2. **Build a traceable lot history:** exact auction event, lot, model/serial/configuration, observed amount, date, sale status and source terms.
3. **Normalize actual costs:** paid hammer or acquisition amount, buyer premium, applicable taxes, payment fees, loadout, freight, recon and holding where verified. Missing material components must remain unknown.
4. **Recover matched completed sales:** actual resale proceeds and selling expenses attributable to the exact acquired unit, not retail asking prices.
5. **Calibrate one non-powered attachment category** using sufficiently representative and independently attributable results.
6. **Test against a real buyer's workflow** with consent and no autonomous purchase authority. Measure repeated use, time saved, false HOLDs and missed evidence.
7. **Keep powered equipment, cars and separate field-service R&D** out of the initial live-buying scope until appropriate independent category validation.
